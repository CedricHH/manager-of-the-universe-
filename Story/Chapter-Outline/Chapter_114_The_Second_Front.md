## Hook
Victor stands in the Central Bank's communication hub, a relic of the old kingdom. He’s looking at the map of Terra-Solvaris not as a battlefield, but as a "Market with too much consolidated competition." He needs to diversify the risk.

## Development
### Beat 1: The Legitimist "Consultation"
Victor uses a pre-war Royal Guard encryption (provided by Dravik) to contact Princess Celestia. He doesn't ask for help; he provides a "Market Opportunity." He gives her the exact coordinates of the Thornfield Junction supply depot—the throat of the Revolutionary supply line. If she hits it, she gains legitimacy; if she doesn't, she starves. She accepts.

### Beat 2: The Theocratic Incentive
Victor contacts High Priest Mordecai of the Theocrats. He doesn't offer a miracle; he offers a "Lost Asset Recovery." He provides intel on the "Altar of Solara," deep in the east of the city, currently held by a small Revolutionary garrison. Mordecai, blinded by holy greed, commits his paladins.

### Beat 3: The Three-Front Reallocation
Victor monitors the troop movements via ARMI. As the Legitimists hit Thornfield and the Theocrats surge toward Solvara East, Cassian Vex is forced to split his 25,000-man army. He can’t let the supplies burn or the holy site fall. He reallocates 10,000 troops away from Ironhold. Victor’s goal: 25k → 15k threat.

## Cliffhanger
The strategy is working, but Sera pulls Victor to a side scrying monitor. Dren Kieran isn't just double-dealing arms anymore. He’s actively sabotaging the anti-siege ballistas on the northern wall—planting "mana-feedback" runes that will cause them to explode when fired. He’s not just a traitor; he’s an active threat.
