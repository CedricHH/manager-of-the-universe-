## Hook
The Revolutionary vanguard emerges from the Iron Woods at dawn—5,000 troops in disciplined blocks. The Merchant mercenaries at Ironhold start to panic; command friction begins immediately.

## Development
### Beat 1: The Tactical Liaison
Dravik and the Merchant lords are arguing over who commands the wall. Victor interrupts. He doesn't take command; he creates a "Tactical Liaison" role. He tells them: "I won't give orders. I will provide 'Actionable Intelligence Analytics.' You have 34% better survival if you follow the data pings on your ARMI HUDs."

### Beat 2: The Kill Zones
As the Revolutionary blocks charge, Victor activates the "Kill Zone Analytics." He directs Dravik’s mages and Morthane’s archers to fire at the exact "structural inefficiencies" in the Revolutionary formation identified by Sera. The disciplined blocks are shredded before they reach the moat. Victor's ROI calculation: 2,100 casualties for 340 coalition losses.

### Beat 3: The Sub-Tribune’s Fall
Sub-Tribune Aldric Thorne (Lv15) breaches the western wall with a specialized "Riot Control" spell. Resh Valthor engages him. It’s not a fight; it’s a "Liquidation." Resh defeats Thorne in 23 seconds, taking his officer’s insignia as a "Claim Asset."

## Cliffhanger
The vanguard retreats, but the victory is short-lived. Sera detects a massive mana-wake on the horizon. Cassian Vex has seen the failure of his vanguard. He’s accelerating the main army—25,000 troops. Deployment ETA: 36 hours. No more probing.
