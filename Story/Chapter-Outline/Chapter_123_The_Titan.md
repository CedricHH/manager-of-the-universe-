## Hook
The first wave hits the Titan's defensive perimeter. The Construction Drones swarm like hornets, each one a 10-foot spider made of steel and murder. Mages blast them apart, but there are always more. And behind the drones, the Titan stands motionless, charging its main cannon.

## Development
### Beat 1: The Kill Zone
Victor designates the "Engagement Corridor"—a 200-meter stretch where the Titan's beam can't hit without also destroying the World-Engine's power conduits. The coalition funnels troops into this corridor, using the Architect's own infrastructure as a shield. Losses drop from 40% to 12%.

### Beat 2: The Breach Team
Resh leads a 50-man strike team to the Titan's legs. Sera provides real-time weak-point analysis via ARMI. They discover the Titan's knee joints are plated but not reinforced—built for intimidation, not prolonged combat. Resh calls for mages to focus-fire on the left knee while he attacks the right.

### Beat 3: The Fall
The Titan's left leg buckles. It kneels. For a moment, victory seems possible. Then the Titan's torso rotates 180 degrees, and its back-mounted launchers fire 40 seeker missiles into the coalition rear lines. Vex's command tent is vaporized.

## Cliffhanger
Vex emerges from the rubble, scorched and furious. He grabs the radio: "You used us as a distraction! You let my men die!" Victor responds calmly: "You're alive. The Titan is kneeling. And we have 8 hours left. Are you in or out?"
