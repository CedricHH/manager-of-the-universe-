## Hook
Three days after Steelheart's arrest, twelve Mining Guild ships enter Ferros-9 orbit. Guildmaster Torvald Ironhammer demands Victor hand over the planet within 24 hours or face "economic sanctions." Victor reviews the Guild's claims: they hold a 200-year-old charter from the Old Empire. Legally dubious. Militarily concerning.

## Development
### Beat 1: The Jurisdictional Challenge
Victor challenges the charter's validity: the Old Empire dissolved 150 years ago, and the Consortium has galactic jurisdiction over "distressed assets." He files a counter-claim with the Consortium Arbitration Board. Torvald responds by blockading the planet—no ships in or out.

### Beat 2: The Workers' Choice
The miners are trapped on-planet with 3 weeks of supplies. Torvald expects them to riot and force Victor out. Instead, Victor opens the emergency stockpiles (Steelheart's hidden reserves) and distributes them. He also offers the miners a choice: stay and become employees of Insolvia Mining (with profit-sharing), or take severance and leave when the blockade lifts.

### Beat 3: The Architect's Move
The Architect contacts Victor: "The Guild has 12 ships. I have one. But mine is bigger." The *Foundry* decloaks above Ferros-9. The Guild ships scatter. Torvald's communication comes through: "This isn't over. The Consortium may protect you now, but we have allies. Powerful ones."

## Cliffhanger
Kira materializes in Victor's office. "The Board flagged this incident. The Guild filed a formal complaint. You're being summoned *again*—but this time, they're questioning your partnership with #888. Someone on the Board wants you gone."
