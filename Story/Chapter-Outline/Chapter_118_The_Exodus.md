## Hook
The broadcast goes live at dawn. Not a speech, but a ledger. ARMI projects a holographic balance sheet over the Revolutionary camp: "Current Daily Wage: 2 coppers (Unpaid for 3 weeks). Victor's Offer: 200 GP Signing Bonus + Hot Shower." The effect is instant.

## Development
### Beat 1: The Walking Ledger
The first wave isn't a trickle; it's a flood. 400 soldiers drop their weapons and walk across no-man's-land. Vex orders his archers to fire. They refuse. The economic disparity has broken the chain of command. Victor watches from Ironhold: "Loyalty has a price. Hunger has a lower one."

### Beat 2: The Logistics of Mercy
Ironhold wasn't built for 5,000 guests. The bunker becomes a processing center. Victor creates "The Airlock"—a three-stage vetting process managed by Sera (Mind Scan), Resh (Physical Search), and Sniv (Bureaucracy/Tags). The challenge isn't the war anymore; it's sanitation, food, and housing. The "Defector Program" threatens to bankrupt their supplies before the enemy attacks.

### Beat 3: The Tyrant's Response
Cassian Vex rallies his elite "Red Guard" (fanatics who can't be bought). He executes three officers on live broadcast to stop the bleeding. He declares the defectors "Infected" and orders the camp burned to prevent anyone else from leaving. The Revolutionaries aren't an army anymore; they are a hostage situation.

## Cliffhanger
Sera pulls Victor aside at the processing station. "We have a problem. Defector #417. His biological reading is... wrong." Victor looks at the scan. The soldier isn't human. He's a construct wrapped in flesh. The "Internal Rival" has sent a present.
