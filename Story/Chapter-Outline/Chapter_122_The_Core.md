## Hook
The World-Engine isn't a drill. It's a straw. Victor watches as the glowing spire extends toward the planet's crust, and ARMI starts screaming. "PLANETARY MANA EXTRACTION IN PROGRESS. ESTIMATED DEPLETION: 72 HOURS." The Architect isn't stealing the Bank. He's stealing the *planet*.

## Development
### Beat 1: The Galactic Real Estate Flip
Kira explains: The Architect doesn't conquer planets. He *hollows* them. Drains the mana, sells the refined energy on the Galactic Exchange, then leaves the husk. Terra-Solvaris is worth 12M GP on paper, but its Core is worth 2.4 *billion* GP in raw mana. Victor's mission was always bait: save the Bank, ignore the real asset.

### Beat 2: The Counter-Proposal
Victor can't stop the extraction. But he can make it *expensive*. He opens a line to the Architect directly. "You're extracting 2.4 billion GP. My coalition holds the only stable landing zone. You pay us a toll—1%—or we make the extraction fail." The Architect laughs: "You have 72 hours of leverage. I have eternity."

### Beat 3: The Sabotage Option
Resh proposes the only solution: destroy the World-Engine before it reaches the Core. But the Engine is guarded by the Titan. And the Titan is guarded by an army of Construction Drones. Victor calculates: they need to kill the Titan in 12 hours, or the Engine will be too deep to destroy. He authorizes the Full Coalition Assault.

## Cliffhanger
The unified army charges. Mages, soldiers, mercenaries, and former enemies all run toward the same target. The Titan raises its arm. A beam of light cuts through the front ranks. The assault has begun—and the casualties have already started.
