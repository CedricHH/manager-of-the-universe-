## Hook
The Revolutionary main force (15,000) hits the northern gate with the force of a tidal wave. They expect a mutiny; they find a meat-grinder. The air is thick with the scent of ozone and iron.

## Development
### Beat 1: The Trap Sprung
The Revolutionary chargers breach the outer northern ward, cheering for the "sabotage" Kieran promised. Instead, Victor activates the "Delayed Resolution" protocol. The ballistas Kieran "sabotaged" were actually recalibrated to fire "Shatter-Bolts" onto specific structural points in the gatehouse, causing it to collapse *onto* the Revolutionary vanguard. 2,500 casualties in the first 10 minutes.

### Beat 2: The Eastern Breach
Vex realizes the trap and redirects a elite contingent to the eastern wall, which was thinned for the northern defense. A breach is made. Resh Valthor arrives. It’s a 4-minute slaughter. Resh clears the gap single-handedly, sustaining 67 casualties while he personally liquidates 12 Revolutionary mages.

### Beat 3: The CEO vs the Tribune
Cassian Vex engages personally, using a massive "Dispersed Barrier" to shield his remaining troops. Victor directs the "Synchronized Resource Reallocation." He doesn't fight Vex; he drains him. He forces the Coalition mages to fire non-lethal mana-siphons until Vex’s barrier flickers. Resh closes the distance and delivers a precision strike. Vex is wounded again, his resolve breaking.

## Cliffhanger
Vex signals the full retreat. The Revolutionary army pulls back into the Solvara ruins, leaving thousands of dead behind. The siege is lifted. Victor checks the ARMI log: 4,200 Revolutionary casualties (28%) vs 890 Coalition (14.5%). But the victory is only a pause. "We have 72 to 96 hours," Victor says. "Before he stops being a Tribune and starts being a desperation-crazed warlord."
