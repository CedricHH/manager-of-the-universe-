## Hook
Victor steps through the dimensional gate back to Oakhaven. The dungeon entrance looks... different. There's a banner: "Welcome Back, Boss." Sniv is waiting with a formal report binder. "Boss! Sniv has good news! Revenue up 47%. Zero fatalities. Three new floors operational. Also, small fire in Floor 6. But Sniv put it out!"

## Development
### Beat 1: The Audit
Victor tours the dungeon. Floor 6 is a disaster (the "small fire" was a dragon food incident), but Floors 7-9 are pristine. Nova reports the Core expansion is ahead of schedule. Marcus shows Victor the apothecary numbers: 12,000 GP profit in 4 weeks. Lysander has successfully mediated three Guild disputes. The system *worked*.

### Beat 2: The Surprise
Asterion pulls Victor aside: "We have a visitor. Arrived three days ago. Refused to leave." Victor enters his office to find Lira sitting in his chair, reading his contracts. She's been studying the ARMI interface. "You said I could be an intern. I'm ready to start."

### Beat 3: The Expansion
Victor convenes his leadership team. The Terra-Solvaris mission is complete, but it revealed opportunities: dimensional trade, multi-planet operations, energy partnerships. He proposes the next phase: expand Insolvia Holdings into a multi-system corporation. First target: establish a second dungeon on a nearby planet.

## Cliffhanger
A courier arrives with a sealed letter bearing the Architect's sigil. Inside: coordinates to a "distressed asset" on a mining planet, and a proposal—60/40 split on acquisition and restructuring. Victor smiles: "Gentlemen, we're going corporate raiding."
