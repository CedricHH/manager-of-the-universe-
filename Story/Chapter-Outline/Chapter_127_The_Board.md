## Hook
The dimensional gate opens onto Orbital Platform Sigma—but not the observation deck Victor visited before. This is the Board Chamber: a circular room floating in the void, surrounded by holographic screens showing the economic output of 47 star systems. Twelve chairs. Eleven occupied. The twelfth is for Victor.

## Development
### Beat 1: The Charges
Executor Veyra reads the charges: "Unauthorized partnership formation with Restructurer #888. Deviation from mission parameters. Planetary asset retention without Board approval." Victor responds: "I saved 2.4 billion GP in planetary mana, secured a 60/40 trade partnership, and established a permanent Consortium presence on Terra-Solvaris. The deviation was profitable."

### Beat 2: The Defense
The Board argues Victor's partnership sets a dangerous precedent: Restructurers working together undermines the competitive evaluation system. Victor counter-argues: "The system is designed to identify talent. I demonstrated talent. So did #888. You don't evaluate employees by making them fight to the death." The Architect's hologram materializes: "I concur. The partnership model is... intriguing."

### Beat 3: The Vote
The Board deliberates. Victor watches the screens: his Oakhaven operations are still running (Marcus sends a status report mid-hearing). The vote comes down to 6-5. Conditional approval: the partnership can continue, but Victor is assigned a permanent observer (Kira). Any future collaborations require pre-approval.

## Cliffhanger
As Victor leaves the chamber, Kira pulls him aside. "You won. But you should know—three Board members abstained. They're planning something else. Watch your back."
