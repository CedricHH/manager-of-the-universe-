## Hook
The Architect's voice fills the core chamber. "10% is... insufficient. But I appreciate the creativity. Let me make a counter-offer." A holographic contract materializes in front of Victor. The terms are simple: the Architect stops the extraction in exchange for 25% of Victor's *personal* Consortium equity, plus... Victor.

## Development
### Beat 1: The Terms
The Architect doesn't want gold; he wants Victor's *contract*. If Victor agrees, he becomes the Architect's asset for one year—a consultant on future planetary acquisitions. In exchange: the child is freed, the World-Engine withdraws, and Terra-Solvaris is spared. Victor reads the fine print: indentured servitude with a golden cage.

### Beat 2: The Rejection
Victor refuses. "You're offering me employment. I'm offering you a *partnership*." He modifies the contract on the spot: instead of selling himself, he proposes a joint venture. The Architect keeps the mana he's already extracted (~5%), Victor keeps the planet, and they split future energy trade 60/40. "You don't need to hollow planets. You need *clients*."

### Beat 3: The Gamble
The Architect is silent for 47 seconds. Then: "You're either brilliant or suicidal. I accept—provisionally. Withdraw the World-Engine as a gesture of good faith. If the coalition breaks the ceasefire, I resume extraction." The Titan powers down. The child goes limp. Resh catches her before she falls. Victor exhales.

## Cliffhanger
The World-Engine retracts. The *Foundry* ship rises back into the sky. Victor has saved the planet—but he's just become the Architect's business partner. Kira materializes beside him, her face unreadable: "You just made a deal with #888. The Board will want to discuss this."
