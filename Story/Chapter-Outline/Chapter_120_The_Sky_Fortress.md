## Hook
The sky camouflage falls away like shattered glass. The *Foundry* hangs in the air—a blocky, industrial dreadnought that makes the Ironhold bunker look like a toy. It doesn't fire lasers; it fires *buildings*. Drop-pods slam into the earth, unfolding into walls, turrets, and factories instantly. The Architect isn't invading; he's rezoning.

## Development
### Beat 1: The Zoning Violation
Victor analyzes the deployment. The Architect is building a "Siege Ring" around the Bank. Within 6 hours, Ironhold will be cut off from the rest of the world. "He's putting us in a cage," Victor realizes. "If that wall closes, he starves us out."

### Beat 2: The Enemy of My Enemy
Victor opens a channel to Tribune Vex on the unsecured frequency. "Cassian. Look up. That ship just crushed three of your battalions. Do you want to be the King of a crater?" Vex is hesitant, but when a Construction Drone (spider-tank) vaporizes his command tent, the logic becomes undeniable. A Ceasefire is signed in 30 seconds.

### Beat 3: The Combined Assault
It's a chaotic mess. Revolutionary mages blast the unfolding walls while Dravik's artillery pounds the drop zones. Victor coordinates the chaos via ARMI, treating the two armies like separate departments in a merger. Resh leads a strike team to breach the "East Wall" before it solidifies.

## Cliffhanger
They breach the wall, but the *Foundry* responds. A massive drop-pod slams down in the breach. It unfolds into a Tier 4 Siegebreaker—a 30-foot tall golem made of fortress plating. The Architect speaks over the global channel: "Eviction Notice Served. You have 24 hours to vacate the coordinates."
