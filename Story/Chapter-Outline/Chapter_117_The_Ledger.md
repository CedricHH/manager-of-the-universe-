## Hook
Three days after the siege, the soot is still being washed from the walls of Ironhold. Victor stands inside the deepest vault of the Central Bank, but he isn't looking at gold. He’s looking at a ledger that predates the war by a thousand years.

## Development
### Beat 1: The Intangible Jackpot
Victor activates a deep ARMI scan on the Royal Archives. He explains to Dravik that gold is just "Hardware." The archive—the deeds, the laws, the birthrights—is the "Software" of Terra-Solvaris. ARMI revalues the assets at 847M GP. "With this," Victor says, "we don't just win a war. We execute a total market reset."

### Beat 2: The Defector Program
Victor proposes a "Human Capital Acquisition" plan. Instead of fighting the remaining 20,000 Revolutionary soldiers, he wants to buy them. He allocates 1M GP for a "Defector Program"—200 GP per soldier who swaps sides. Sera confirms morale is at 12%; the offer is a "Statistical Certainty" to trigger a mass migration.

### Beat 3: The Kira Check-in
Kira manifests in the vault, her holographic form flickering. She isn't there to congratulate him. She warns Victor: "The Board is watching. Your 23-day record and now the Solvara stabilization have made you a target. Rivals are concerned you’re disrupting the 'Natural Progression' of the star system." She hints at another Restructurer being "compensated" to ensure his failure.

## Cliffhanger
Kira vanishes, leaving Victor with a mission clock that now feels like a countdown to an assassination attempt. Resh enters, his draconic eyes narrowed. "Someone just breached the orbital ward, Victor. And they didn't use a Consortium gate. They used a back-door protocol." Victor realizes: The threat isn't outside the walls anymore. It’s coming from above.
