## Hook
The Siegebreaker (Titan) stops 100 meters from the wall. It doesn't attack. It just stands there, a 30-foot monolith of silent intimidation. Victor uses the pause to invoke "Clause 11" of the Emergency Governance Act (which he just wrote). He calls a meeting. Not a parley—a Shareholder Meeting.

## Development
### Beat 1: The Boardroom in the Bunker
Victor gathers the leaders—Dravik (in person), Vex (via captured radio), Celestia (via magical projection), and Valorian (via courier message). He puts the Architect's "Eviction Notice" on the screen. "He's not asking for surrender," Victor explains. "He's rezoning the planet. You aren't kings fighting for a throne anymore. You're squatters."

### Beat 2: The Equity Split
Victor proposes the "Solvaris Defense Pact." It's not a treaty; it's a corporation.
- Revolutionaries: 30% Equity (Manpower)
- Warlords: 20% Equity (The Bank/Fortress)
- Legitimists: 15% Equity (Legitimacy/Magic)
- Merchants: 15% Equity (Supply Lines)
- Victor: 20% Equity (Management/Tech)
Vex objects to working with Dravik. Victor points to the Titan outside. "The market has changed. Consolidate or be liquidated."

### Beat 3: The Vote
The Titan takes a step forward. The ground shakes. The debating stops. One by one, they sign (or verbally agree). It's the first time in 20 years the factions have agreed on anything. Victor activates the "Defense Pact" protocol in his ARMI. A specialized "Alliance Buff" (purchased for 50k GP) washes over the combined armies.

## Cliffhanger
The newly formed "Solvaris Union" army moves to intercept the Titan. But as they engage, the Architect's ship opens its main bay. It's not sending more troops. It's lowering a "World-Engine"—a massive drill designed to core the planet. The eviction was just a distraction. He's here for the Core.
