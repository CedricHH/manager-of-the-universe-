## Hook
The Merchant Coalition delegation arrives at Ironhold in armored carriages, a stark contrast to the muddy ruins outside. Victor stands at the gates, not as a dungeoneer, but as a Consortium Lead.

## Development
### Beat 1: The Death Mark Proof
Negotiations begin in a damaged boardroom. The Merchants are hesitant to invest in a "sinking ship." Victor stands and unbuttons his collar, revealing the glowing golden glyph of the Clause 47-Delta death contract. "The Consortium doesn't bet on losers," he says. "If I fail, my heart stops. I have a vested interest in your ROI."

### Beat 2: The Double Dealer
Sera sends a private update to Victor's ARMI: she's traced a hidden mana signature from Dren Kieran’s luggage to a Revolutionary supply drop. Kieran is selling "defense intel" to Cassian Vex. Victor doesn't call him out publicly; he uses it for "Leverage Acquisition."

### Beat 3: The Leverage Play
Victor pulls Kieran aside for a "private consultation." He shows him the scrying data. "You can be liquidated for treason, or you can reduce your coalition equity from 25% to 15% and commit your personal mercenary force to the front line." Kieran, pale, chooses the latter.

## Cliffhanger
The Merchants sign the provisional agreement for 4,000 mercenaries. But as the ink dries, a Warlord scout bursts in. The Revolutionary vanguard—5,000 troops—has been spotted pushing through the Iron Woods. ETA: 18 hours.
