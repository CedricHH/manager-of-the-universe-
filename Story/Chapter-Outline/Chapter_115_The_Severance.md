## Hook
Victor and Resh corner Dren Kieran in the north wall ballista workshop. Kieran is holding a mana-rune—a literal smoking gun. Kieran doesn't beg; he tries to negotiate. Victor laughs.

## Development
### Beat 1: The Severance Package
Victor presents Kieran with a "Severance Agreement." He doesn't execute him; he "repurposes his function." The deal: Face Resh’s blades now, or send a specific message to Cassian Vex. Kieran is forced to commit his remaining 1,000 mercenaries to the defense and reduce his equity stake to 15%. Resh stands behind him, blades unsheathed, as the "Performance Penalty."

### Beat 2: The False Intel
Under Victor's supervision, Kieran sends a priority transmission to Vex. The message: "Northern approach vulnerable. Sabotage successful. Internal guards in mutiny." In reality, Victor has concentrated Dravik’s elite guard and the Merchant mercenaries at the northern gate, creating a meat-grinder.

### Beat 3: The Split
Sera monitors the main Revolutionary army. Vex, receiving Kieran's "intel" and under pressure from the Legitimists and Theocrats, splits his force again. He sends 15,000 to the "vulnerable" northern gate of Ironhold, while the rest are bogged down on the other fronts. Victor’s math: 30k → 15k threat.

## Cliffhanger
The Revolutionary army—15,000 strong—is visible from the top of the northern wall. They aren't marching; they’re charging, convinced of an internal mutiny. Victor watches the ARMI countdown. 15,000 is still 3 to 1 odds. "Let them come," he says. "The market is about to experience a correction."
