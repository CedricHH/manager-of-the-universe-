## Hook
Victor sits in his Oakhaven office, reviewing the numbers: Dungeon Insolvia (8k GP/week), Terra-Solvaris Partnership (2% of 2.4B GP over time), Ferros-9 Mining (recovering 8M GP). He's built an empire. And empires attract enemies. Kira arrives with intelligence: "Board Member #7. Restructurer #003. He's been undermining you since the Architect partnership."

## Development
### Beat 1: The Rival
Kira reveals Restructurer #003: known as "The Accountant," he's held a Board seat for 40 years and controls 23% of Consortium contracts. He views Victor's rapid rise as a threat. His strategy: force Victor into regulatory violations, then terminate his contract. The second summons is a trap.

### Beat 2: The Counter-Strategy
The Architect proposes a solution: instead of defending at the hearing, Victor should *expand*. Acquire more assets, generate more profit, become too valuable to terminate. "The Board respects one thing: results. Give them results they can't ignore."

### Beat 3: The Portfolio
Victor consolidates his holdings into a formal corporate structure: **Kaine Universal Holdings**. Subsidiaries include Dungeon Insolvia, Solvaris Energy Partners, Ferros Mining Operations, and the Galactic Arbitrage Division. He files the incorporation with the Consortium—making his operations official, transparent, and *protected* under corporate law.

## Cliffhanger
Victor boards the dimensional gate to Orbital Platform Sigma for his second Board hearing. But this time, he's not going as a defendant. He's going as the CEO of a multi-billion GP corporation. As he steps through, Lira hands him a datapad: "First quarter projections. You'll want these." The numbers are staggering.
