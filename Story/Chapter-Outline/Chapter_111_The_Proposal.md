## Hook
Victor stands on the balcony of Ironhold, overlooking the smoking ruins of the Revolutionary vanguard. He isn't looking at the dead; he's looking at the logistical inefficiency of the retreat.

## Development
### Beat 1: The Coalition Math
Victor meets with Dravik. He presents a "Spreadsheet of Sovereignty." He explains that while Dravik has the gold, he lacks the "Market Cap" to survive 30,000 troops. Victor proposes a Coalition—not out of friendship, but as a "Leveraged Buyout" of the political landscape.

### Beat 2: The Civilization Backup
Dravik, impressed by the defense in Ch 113, takes Victor deep into the Central Bank vault. It's not just bars of gold. Dravik reveals the archives: land deeds for every province, noble genealogies that prove legitimacy, and trade treaties that have been "distressed" by the war. Victor realizes the Bank is the *operating system* of Terra-Solvaris.

### Beat 3: The ARMI Revaluation
Victor's ARMI system scan flashes. The 16.7M GP is just the "Liquid Capital." The archives represent 847M GP in "Intangible Assets." He tells Dravik: "You aren't a Warlord; you're a Custodian of a failing brand. It's time to relaunch."

## Cliffhanger
Sera enters, her face unusually pale. She’s intercepted a high-level Revolutionary transmission. Tribune Vex isn’t waiting 72 hours. He’s liquidating his other fronts to concentrate everything on Ironhold. The ultimatum just dropped from 72 hours to 48.
