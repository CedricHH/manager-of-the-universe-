## Hook
The Titan's chest cavity is open—a gaping wound carved by Resh's blade. Victor stands at the edge, looking into the mechanical abyss. "It's a dungeon," he says. "Just... vertical." He steps inside. The walls hum with power. And somewhere deep within, something is *watching*.

## Development
### Beat 1: The Mechanical Labyrinth
The Titan's interior is a maze of pistons, conduits, and automated defenses. Resh carves a path while Sera maps the layout. Victor notices the construction pattern: this Titan wasn't built—it was *grown*. Bio-mechanical. The Architect doesn't build machines; he builds *organisms*.

### Beat 2: The Pilot
Sera's scans reveal a life sign at the core. Not a power source—a *person*. Someone is piloting the Titan from within. Victor asks Kira (via ARMI): "Is the Architect physically present?" Kira responds: "Negative. But #888 has been known to use... contractors." The pilot is a slave, not an enemy.

### Beat 3: The Negotiation
They reach the core chamber. The pilot is a child—12 years old, wired into the Titan's nervous system, unable to move. The Architect's voice echoes: "Impressive, Restructurer. You got here faster than I expected. But can you kill a child to save a planet?" Victor stares at the kid. The mission clock shows 6 hours.

## Cliffhanger
Victor activates ARMI's Contract function. "I'm not going to kill you," he says to the child. "I'm going to *buy* you." He makes an offer to the Architect: 10% of the Consortium investment in exchange for the pilot's release and Titan deactivation. The Architect pauses. "...Interesting."
