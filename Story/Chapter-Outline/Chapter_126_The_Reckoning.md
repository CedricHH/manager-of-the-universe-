## Hook

The sun rises over Terra-Solvaris for the first time in three days without an orbital shadow. The Foundry is gone. The coalition soldiers sit in the rubble, staring at the sky. Victor stands in the Bank Vault, reviewing the final casualty count: 4,800 dead. 12,000 wounded. And somehow, victory.

## Development

### Beat 1: The Reckoning

Vex storms into the Bank Vault, demanding answers. "You made a deal with that monster. You sold us out." Victor shows him the contract: no territorial concessions, no tribute, no servitude. The Architect got 5% of already-extracted mana (worth ~120M GP) and a non-compete clause. "We didn't lose anything we had. We kept everything that mattered."

### Beat 2: The New Order

Victor convenes the Defense Pact leaders. The ceasefire is now a treaty. The terms: the Bank becomes a shared asset (Consortium-managed, faction-owned), trade routes are reopened, and a Provisional Council is established with rotating leadership. Vex abstains. Celestia signs. Valorian signs. Dravik signs. The war is over.

### Beat 3: The Ward

Victor visits Lira in the medical wing. She's 12 years old, kidnapped from a backwater planet, wired into a war machine for 8 months. She has no family, no home, no future. Victor offers her a choice: he can arrange transport to anywhere in the star system, or she can stay. "I need a new intern," he says. She doesn't smile, but she nods.

## Cliffhanger

Kira appears as Victor leaves the medical wing. "The Board is convening in 72 hours. Your partnership with #888 requires formal review. If they don't approve, both of you will be... restructured." Victor straightens his tie: "Then I'd better prepare a very good pitch."
