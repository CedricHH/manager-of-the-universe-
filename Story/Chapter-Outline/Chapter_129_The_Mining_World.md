## Hook
Planet Ferros-9: a gray, barren rock where 40,000 miners dig for Mana Crystals. Victor lands at the spaceport to find the workers on strike. The mining director, Varn Steelheart, is selling 30% of extracted crystals on the black market and blaming "operational inefficiencies." The Architect's message was clear: fix this, 60/40 split of recovered assets.

## Development
### Beat 1: The Investigation
Victor deploys Lira to audit the mine's systems. She discovers falsified production logs: output is reported at 70%, but actual extraction is 110%. The missing 40% is being siphoned. Steelheart has accumulated 8M GP in hidden assets over 3 years. The Consortium has been paying him bonuses for "efficiency."

### Beat 2: The Workers
Victor meets with the strike leaders. They're not asking for higher wages—they're asking for safety equipment. Steelheart has been cutting corners: 47 deaths in 6 months. Victor offers a deal: end the strike, provide testimony against Steelheart, and receive profit-sharing (5% of recovered assets distributed among workers).

### Beat 3: The Confrontation
Victor confronts Steelheart with the evidence. Steelheart tries to bribe him (2M GP). Victor refuses. Steelheart threatens violence. Lira activates a hidden recording: every word documented. Victor smiles: "You just threatened a Consortium Restructurer. That's Clause 73-Omega. Immediate asset seizure."

## Cliffhanger
Consortium security arrests Steelheart. But as they drag him away, he shouts: "You think you're the hero? Wait until the Guild finds out you're stealing their planets!" Victor freezes. The Architect warned him: there's a Mining Guild that considers Ferros-9 their territory.
