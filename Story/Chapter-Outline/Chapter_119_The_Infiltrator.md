## Hook
Sera's scanner screams. Defector #417 isn't breathing. He isn't bleeding. He's *transmitting*. Victor orders the bunker sealed, but Kira stops him. "It's not a spy, Victor. It's a coordinates lock. You're being painted for a strike."

## Development
### Beat 1: The Biology of Betrayal
Resh dissects the "soldier" on a table. Under the skin, it's all mana-circuitry—Tier 4 biomimicry. Kira recognizes the signature: Restructurer #888, "The Architect." A legend in the Consortium for "Structural Reintegration" (which usually means leveling a city and rebuilding it). He's not here to compete economically; he's here to demolish the competition.

### Beat 2: The Coordinates
Sera traces the transmission. It's targeting the Bank Vault specifically. The orbital strike isn't explosive; it's a "Void-Anchor" designed to teleport the entire vault contents into orbit. The Architect wants to steal the 16.7M GP asset in one move. Time to lock: 14 minutes.

### Beat 3: The Hot Potato
Victor can't stop the signal, so he decides to move the target. He authorizes Resh to run Defector #417's body to the Revolutionary frontline. "If he wants to steal a vault," Victor says, "let's give him a bomb instead." They strap a Mana-Overload Charge (purchased from Exchange, 5k GP) to the construct.

## Cliffhanger
Resh drops the body 500 meters from Vex's command tent and sprints back. The sky splits open. A beam of blue light hits the construct. But instead of teleporting it, the Mana-Overload Charge detonates inside the beam, feeding back into the Architect's ship. The sky *cracks*.
