# The Manager of the Universe
## Arc 1: Dead Capital

When Victor Kaine awakens next to a dead hero in a dungeon, he doesn't reach for a sword. He reaches for a contract.

Armed with the ARMI System—a cosmic spreadsheet that treats reality like a balance sheet—Victor transforms the dungeon into a business. Goblins become employees. Monsters get dental plans. The minotaur signs an entertainment contract.

But Victor's true past haunts him: A corporate manager who exposed corruption and died for it. Now reincarnated in a fantasy world, he sees the same inefficiencies everywhere—corrupt guilds, bankrupt kingdoms, and gods who hoard resources like incompetent executives.

From zero gold to a million in record time. From dungeon administrator to economic overlord. Victor doesn't conquer with fireballs—he conquers with leverage, hostile takeovers, and weaponized bureaucracy.

**Arc 1** follows Victor's transformation from desperate survivor to established tycoon, managing a universe that desperately needs restructuring.

### What to Expect:

- **Smart protagonist**: Victor solves problems with economics, not violence
- **System-based progression**: ARMI tracks everything from performance reviews to hostile acquisitions
- **Corporate warfare in fantasy**: Guilds, banks, and churches face their worst nightmare—a competent manager
- **Anti-hero with principles**: Victor exploits systems, not people
- **LitRPG meets business strategy**: Management skills are literal magic

**The universe has negative ROI. Time for a hostile takeover.**
