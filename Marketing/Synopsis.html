Der Manager des Universums

Victor Kaine war bekannt als „Die Kettensäge" – ein gefürchteter Interim-Manager, der Fortune-500-Unternehmen mit chirurgischer Präzision zerlegte. Kein Mitarbeiter war sicher. Kein Budget heilig. Kein Gefühl relevant.

Ihre Diagnose: Die Welt Terra-Insolvia hat negative Rendite. Restrukturierung oder Liquidation.
Ihre Lösung: Ein Manager, der Systeme versteht, ausnutzt und… einmal, nur einmal, etwas über den Profit gestellt hat.

Victor erwacht neben der Leiche des „Auserwählten Helden". In seinen Händen: kein Schwert. Nur eine System-Nachricht:

[ARMI System initialisiert]
Klasse zugewiesen: Asset Manager (Einzigartig)
Startkapital: 0 Gold
Empfehlung: Sofortige Erlösgenerierung zur Vermeidung von Insolvenz

Während andere Protagonisten Monster töten, verhandelt Victor Arbeitsverträge mit Goblins. Während Helden Bosse besiegen, reicht Victor dem Minotaurus Kündigungsschreiben aus HR – und stellt ihn ein.

Willkommen in einer LitRPG-Welt, in der Schulden tödlicher sind als Schwerter, Zinsen schneller töten als Gift, und der wahre Endboss…

Synopsis (Full Version)

The Manager of the Universe

Victor Kaine was known as "The Chainsaw"—a feared interim manager who dismembered Fortune 500 companies with surgical precision. No employee was safe. No budget was sacred. No feeling was relevant.

Then he became the scapegoat. The board discarded him like an unprofitable division.

When Victor died—clutching a USB drive of damning evidence in his hand—the Galactic Audit Consortium collected his soul.

Their diagnosis: The world of Terra-Insolvia has negative ROI. Restructure or liquidate.
Their solution: A manager who understands systems, exploits systems, and—just once—chose something over profit.

Victor wakes next to the corpse of the "Chosen Hero." In his hands: no sword. Just a system notification:

[ARMI System Initialized]
Class assigned: Asset Manager (Unique)
Starting Capital: 0 Gold
Recommendation: Immediate revenue generation to avoid insolvency

While other protagonists slay monsters, Victor negotiates employment contracts with goblins. While heroes defeat bosses, Victor serves the Minotaur HR termination papers—and hires him.

Welcome to a LitRPG world where debt is deadlier than swords, interest kills faster than poison, and the true final boss...

Royal Road (Blurb - 500 words max)

Victor Kaine spent his life as "The Chainsaw"—a corporate restructurer who turned failing companies profitable through sheer ruthlessness. People were spreadsheet rows. Emotions were inefficiencies. Profit was the only metric that mattered.

Then his own board of directors sacrificed him to cover up a scandal involving conflict minerals and dead children. Victor lost everything: his reputation, his career, the cold empire he'd built through decades of calculated cruelty.

But before he died—clutching evidence that would imprison three executives—the Galactic Audit Consortium noticed something in his soul. Maximum efficiency. Maximum exploitation. And one tiny, inexplicable flicker of humanity.

They sent him to Terra-Insolvia. A fantasy world flagged for "Restructuring or Liquidation" due to negative cosmic ROI.

His class? Asset Manager (Unique).
His starting capital? 0 Gold.
His mission? Generate profit or cease to exist.

Now Victor must survive in a dungeon using the only skills he has: negotiation, exploitation, and an encyclopedic knowledge of predatory contract law.

Goblins become employees. The boss monster becomes middle management. The dungeon itself becomes a profitable enterprise.

And this is just the tutorial.

Features:

  • 🧠 Intelligent MC who wins through economics, not combat
  • 📊 Unique "corporate magic" system (skills like [Hostile Takeover], [Predatory Lending], [Liquidate])
  • 🎭 Dark comedy meets genuine character development
  • 🏰 Progression from dungeon → city → kingdom → world → universe
  • ❌ No harem, no dense MC, no chosen one power fantasy
  • 📈 800+ chapter epic planned across 5 arcs

The hero is dead. The manager is here. And he will own everything.
